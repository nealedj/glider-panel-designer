## M1 Front editor

| Acceptance criterion | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Ten parts placed by mouse and keyboard | `e2e/m1-front-editor.spec.ts` places eight parts by library click then canvas click, and two with Ctrl+D. Drag from the library also works. |
| Positions match typed values to 0.01 mm | Typed values for all ten parts, including `2.25in` → 57.15 mm, are stored exactly. The test checks them to < 0.01 mm. A drag with a typed distance of `12.5` moves exactly 12.5 mm. |
| Undo restores every step | The test records the document after each of about 40 steps (place, duplicate, typed X/Y, nudges, rotate, drag), then undoes one step at a time back to the empty project and checks each state is identical. Redo replays them all. |
| Performance target at 200 parts | With 200 parts at 1440 × 900 in Chromium, wheel zoom, pan and part drag all hold a median frame of 16.7 ms, p95 16.8 ms. That is locked to the 60 fps refresh. |

Features:

- Canvas with mm rulers, an adaptive grid, an origin marker, the panel centreline and a view badge ("Front · as the pilot sees it").
- Pan with middle-drag or Space+drag. Wheel zoom centres on the cursor, and trackpad pinch zoom works. F zooms to fit; Shift+F zooms to the selection.
- Select by click, Shift-click and Ctrl-click. Window select works both ways: left→right selects enclosed items, right→left selects anything touched.
- Move by dragging (Alt turns snapping off), with a typed exact distance during the drag, or with M then a base point and a target. Nudge with the arrows: 1 mm, 10 mm with Shift, 0.1 mm with Alt. R rotates 90° and Shift+R rotates −90°. Ctrl+D duplicates and Delete deletes; deleting a part also deletes anything mounted to it.
- Snapping, in order: part centres, cutout, bezel and screw points, then alignment with part edges and centres, template edges and vertices, keep-outs, guides and the centreline, then the grid. Snap lines and markers highlight as you approach. Drag from a ruler to make a guide.
- Arrange: align edges or centres, distribute evenly, mirror copy about the centreline, and linear and circular arrays.
- Inspector: label, X, Y and rotation, which accept unit-suffixed values. It also has lock, the generic part parameters (they rebuild the cutouts live), and footprint, depth and protrusion facts. With nothing selected it shows project properties, the template choice and the grid.
- Keep-out tool: drag a rectangle; the zone is hatched, selectable, movable and editable.
- Measure tool: snapped two-point distance with Δx and Δy.
- Library with search, category filter, favourites, click-to-place and drag-to-place. Objects tree with lock toggles, and layer visibility.
- Checks panel with the data checks: unverified catalogue values, draft template, missing part. Clicking a check selects the parts and zooms to them. A permanent "planning aid only" note sits under it.
- Command palette (Ctrl+K), a menu bar and a shortcut map (?), all driven by one command registry.
- Autosave to IndexedDB and crash recovery on reload (tested). Open and save project file (JSON, validated on open).
- Metric/imperial display toggle (U). Light and dark themes.

Screenshots: `img/m1-placed.png`, `img/m1-positioned.png`, `img/m1-measure.png`, `img/m1-200-parts.png`.

**Check thresholds** have no defaults yet. These project settings are null until set: minimum ligament between cutouts, minimum cutout-to-edge distance, minimum distance from a bend line, and minimum compass distance. M2's front checks need them, and the values depend on the panel material and thickness.

Other defaults chosen: 5 mm snap grid, 8 px snap radius, and a 50 mm wire end allowance per termination (used in M6).
