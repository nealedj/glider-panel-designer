## Spans to measure on the cardboard

Please measure these on the real mock-up with a steel rule and tell me the three numbers.
Or enter them yourself: in the Template tab, open the draft, go to **5 Accuracy**, choose
**Add a check span**, and click the two ends.

| Span | Template says |
| --- | --- |
| A. Widest point of the top section, side to side | 397.5 mm |
| B. Bottom edge of the stem, corner to corner | 139.0 mm |
| C. From the dashed fold line to the top edge, along the centreline | 292.2 mm |
| Optional D. Full height, bottom of the stem to the top edge, along the centreline | 336.5 mm |

Each should agree within ±1.5 mm.

D is optional because the stem hangs off the desk edge in the photo. That part of the card
may not lie flat, so its length is the least certain. If D is off by more than the others,
that is the reason, and the fix is a photo with the whole card on the mat.

## What was delivered

The **Template** tab is a six-step editor. A left panel lists your drafts and the template library.

1. **Photo.**
   - Import a photo or scan, and say what it is: scan, card on a mat, tracing, square-on photo, angled photo, measured or drawn. The angled and square-on choices warn that a scan or a mat photo is far more accurate for cutting.
   - Record the photo's source and rights.
   - The photo is kept in your browser only; it never goes into exports, share links or the repo.
2. **Calibrate.** Four methods:
   - **Mat grid:** found automatically, or from four clicks round one square. Choose what one square is (½ in, 1 in, 10 mm or 5 mm). It straightens the photo, sets the scale and corrects lens distortion. A **template thickness** field corrects for card that sits above the mat (the camera position is estimated from the perspective).
   - **Instruments:** click a centre and a rim; the rim is found, and you give its diameter. Three or more instruments correct for perspective.
   - **Points:** four or more points with known positions.
   - **Scale:** a scan's resolution, or a ruler line of known length.

   Each method reports its residual.
3. **Outline**, on the straightened photo in mm.
   - **Wand:** click inside the panel and it suggests the outline as straight lines and arcs. Colour tolerance and smoothing are adjustable.
   - **Draw:** click the outline yourself.
   - **Edit:** drag vertices (they snap to vertices, alignments and the centreline). Drag an edge's ◆ to make an arc; Alt-click it to straighten. Double-click an edge to add a vertex.
   - **Datum:** click the two bottom corners; the origin goes midway and the panel is levelled.
   - **Mirror left or right half**, for symmetric panels.
4. **Features:**
   - bend lines;
   - fixing holes;
   - keep-outs;
   - material, thickness, free depth, canopy clearance and glider types.
5. **Accuracy:** the estimated error on any distance, with each contributing term shown, and the check spans table.
6. **Save and verify:**
   - Save to your library, or save and use it in the current project. The project then carries the template whole, so files and share links include it.
   - Export the template as JSON, to share it or propose it for the shared library.
   - Print the outline at 1:1, using the M3 template PDF with its check bar.
   - Mark it verified, with a note on how it was checked, and the date.

**Template library:** your own templates appear in the project's template list (marked "yours") next to the built-in ones.

**PIK-20D mock-up template:** `templates/pik20d.t-panel-mockup.json`, a draft with:

- a 30-vertex outline with arcs;
- the fold line;
- free depth left unknown;
- its provenance and method recorded.

**Bug fixed along the way:** a reload within 0.4 s of an edit could lose the latest draft change. Draft saves now run in order and flush when the page is hidden.

**Keyboard:** commands are now scoped to views, so Delete, the arrow keys and the tool keys act on the template editor in the Template tab and on the panel in Front and Rear. The template shortcuts are S, W, O, D, B, H, K, C, I, V and F, plus undo and redo.

**Decisions:** [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-template-pipeline.md).

## Defaults to confirm (rule 11)

| Default | Value | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mock-up card thickness above the mat | **3.5 mm** (typical single-wall corrugated) | It corrects about 1 % of scale, or 4 mm over the panel width. Please measure the card. |
| Rim diameters in the fitted-panel photo | **80 mm** for the ASI, XCVario, mechanical vario and altimeter; **57 mm** for the slip ball, compass and KRT2 | I clicked the black outer rims. If a rim is really, say, 82 mm, everything scales by 2.5 %. A caliper across one 80 mm rim and one 57 mm rim would settle it. |
| Baseline error per input type, for photo effects the residuals cannot see | scan 0.2, card on a mat 0.5, tracing 0.5, square-on photo 1.5, angled photo 3, measured 0.5 mm | These feed the stated error. They are my judgement, not measured values. |
| Field of view assumed when perspective cannot give it | 75° diagonal | It is only used for nearly square-on photos of raised templates. |
| New panel material and thickness | Glass fibre (GRP), 2 mm | Fibreglass is your answer from M2; the thickness is a placeholder until you know it. |

> **Later change (M7 follow-up):** templates no longer hold reserved areas (component
> footprints) or existing cutouts. Parts belong only in the project; see ADR 0010's addendum.

## Not in M4

- **Bézier curves:** outlines use lines and arcs, as the file format does.
- **Depth regions:** templates take one free-depth value for now.
- **Stitching several scans, and richer lens models:** parked in `docs/ideas.md`.
- **The full reference project:** placing the parts on the panel is M7.
