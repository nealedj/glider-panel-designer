# Milestone report: M2 Rear and stacking

2026-10-11

| Acceptance criterion | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A unit mounted behind a long radio shows no clash | `e2e/m2-rear-stacking.spec.ts` places a 57 mm radio 120 mm deep and a behind-panel unit on top of it in plan, then mounts the unit to the radio from the inspector. The unit's band becomes 120–160 mm, the clash disappears, and there is no depth error against the 180 mm free. |
| Moving the radio moves it | The test moves the radio by typed X, by an arrow-key nudge, and by dragging in the mirrored rear view. Each time, the unit's position follows and its offset stays (0, 0, 120). |

Also tested in that flow: the stack inspector (both parts listed deepest first, Tab cycles
the selection), the depth slider (clipping to 125 mm and deeper hides the radio), the front
view (the mounted unit cuts nothing and shows as a hidden outline), and deleting the radio
(also deletes the unit with a note; undo restores both).

Totals: typecheck clean, 85 Vitest tests and 8 Playwright flows pass. The production
build is 128 kB gzip.

## What was delivered

**Rear view.** It is on the Rear tab, or press V to flip between front and rear.

- **Display:**
  - Mirrored x, with the "Rear · viewed from behind" badge.
  - Front cuts as a dashed ghost layer.
  - Bodies shaded by how deep they reach, on a near-to-far ramp with a legend. Deeper parts
    draw on top, as the fitter sees them.
  - Unknown depths are hatched, and clashing parts are outlined in red.
  - Connectors show as small markers: rear-face connectors hollow, side connectors filled.
- **Editing:** select, move, nudge, window select and the inspector all work in the rear
  view.
- **Stack inspector.** Click a point to list every body under it, deepest first, with its
  depth band. Click a row or press Tab to cycle through the stack, so hidden parts can be
  selected.
- **Depth slider.** A two-ended slider on the legend clips the view to a depth range;
  parts outside it dim and cannot be clicked. "Show all depths" resets it. The template's
  free depth is marked on the scale.

**Mounting.**

- **Mounted to.** Set it in the inspector: Panel or any other part, excluding the part
  itself and its descendants.
  - Mounting keeps the part where it is, and starts its depth band just behind the parent's
    rear face.
  - dx, dy and dz are editable. An extra depth offset works for parts on the panel too.
- **Moving and deleting.** Parents carry their children through any depth of nesting.
  Deleting a parent deletes its children and says so. Arrange → "Detach from parent part"
  puts a part back on the panel.
- **No cuts.** A part mounted to another part cuts nothing in the panel.
- **Objects tree.** Children are nested under their parents.
- **New generic part.** "Behind-panel unit" is a box with no cutouts, for FLARM, audio or
  interface units.

**Checks.** These are new geometric checks. Clicking any check zooms to it, and switches
to the rear view for rear checks.

| Area | Check | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front | Cutouts or screw holes of two parts overlap | Error |
| Front | Cut outside the panel outline | Error |
| Front | Cut into a keep-out zone (template or user-drawn) or an existing panel fixing | Error |
| Front | Cut closer to a bend line than the minimum | Error |
| Front | Material between neighbouring cuts below the minimum (screw holes count) | Warning |
| Front | Cut closer to the panel edge than the minimum | Warning |
| Front | Bezels overlap though the cuts do not | Warning |
| Front | Part stands proud of the template's canopy clearance | Warning |
| Rear | Two bodies overlap in plan and in depth | Error |
| Rear | Depth plus rear-connector clearance exceeds the free depth there (template default, or the shallowest depth region the body touches) | Error |
| Rear | Parts with unknown depth are listed as skipped | Warning |

The checks list errors first, then warnings, and design problems before data notes.

**Geometry** (ADR 0005, updated). Circle-to-circle distances are exact. Other shapes are
flattened so that every distance is within 0.01 mm of the true value. No library was
added: the checks need only distances and overlap tests, and no polygon booleans or
offsets. If a later feature needs those, it will bring in `@flatten-js/core` through a new
ADR.

**Depth model** (new ADR 0007). It records:

- the depth band formula
- mounting semantics
- the clash rule: touching in plan or depth is legal
- the available-depth rule.

**Fixes found along the way.**

- Escape in the inspector's Label field cleared the selection; it now only reverts the
  field.
- The command palette kept the previous query when reopened; it now opens empty.

**Performance.** With 200 parts in the rear view, half of them mounted behind the other
half, zoom, pan and drag hold a median frame of 16.7 ms (60 fps). An edit, including all
checks and the redraw, reaches the screen in about 47 ms. The front-view figures from M1
are unchanged.

## Changes to defaults

**57 and 80 mm screw spacing** was changed using the user's photo of the two panel-marking
templates.

- The photo has no scale and some perspective, so I measured the hole spacing relative to
  each template's cutout ring.
- That gives roughly 47–52 mm for the 57 mm template and 60–66 mm for the 80 mm one. The old
  defaults (50 and 70 mm) put the 80 mm holes clearly too far out.
- The new defaults are **47 mm** and **66 mm**. They stay flagged unverified, with a source
  note saying where they came from.
- Please measure across the hole centres on the real templates; ±3 mm is not good enough to
  drill to.

**Front-check thresholds** are set for glass-fibre (GRP) panels, as advised.

- The sample template's material is now "Glass fibre (GRP)". New projects and the sample
  start with these thresholds:

  | Threshold | Value | Reasoning |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Material between cuts | 5 mm | Narrow laminate bridges crack when drilled or when instruments are tightened |
  | Cut to panel edge | 8 mm | Keeps cuts clear of a trimmed edge that often carries the panel's fixings |
  | Cut to bend line | 10 mm | Clears a moulded flange radius plus a margin for the thicker laminate there |

- These are my starting points, not values from a standard or a maker. The Project panel
  says so, and lets you change or clear each one; clearing turns that check off and
  shows an info line.
- Compass distance stays unset; its check arrives with powered parts in M6.

## Not in M2 (by plan or parked)

- Bracket parts named in `mountedTo.bracket` are kept in the file but not drawn or checked.
- Children's offsets do not rotate with a rotated parent.
- There is no editor for template depth regions (the template editor comes in M4).
- Rear labels can overlap when parts stack.
- "A bundle passes through a part body" needs routes, so it comes in M6.

All of these are parked in `docs/ideas.md`.
