# Milestone report: M7 Reference panel and polish

2026-10-14

## Acceptance criterion

> David produces the cut file with the flange bend line, rear layout, wiring and pneumatic
> diagram, wire list and bill of materials for the reference panel (a PIK-20D re-panel)
> without editing JSON by hand, and
> shares it by link.

**Done in software, with placeholder parts.** Only David can carry it out for real, so it
remains his step to take.

A browser test plays David's part, entirely through the interface (`e2e/m7-reference-panel.spec.ts`).
It builds the whole PIK-20D example project from scratch, then makes every output and shares it.

**What the test does:**

1. **New project:** File › New project on the PIK-20D mock-up template, with a name.
2. **Instruments:** each one clicked onto the panel from the parts library, and its
   position typed in the inspector.
3. **Typed values:**
   - the switch and four fuses typed in beside the altimeter on the stem;
   - placeholder sizes for the XCTouchNav, FLARM display and phone mount typed in the
     inspector.
4. **Behind the panel:** the FLARM unit mounted on the back of the XCTouchNav, and three
   tube tees placed.
5. **Off-panel parts**, in the rear view, at the exit point down the stem: the battery,
   main fuse, PTT, antennas, pitot, static, TE probe and speaker.
6. **Wiring:** all 40 wires and tubes connected pin to pin in the schematic.
7. **Looms:** both drawn with the Route tool, and wires ticked onto each.

**The result** is identical to the committed `fixtures/projects/pik20d.json`: parts,
positions, mounting, wires, nets, colours, labels and routes.

**Outputs from that project,** through the export dialog:

| --- | --- |

| Wiring and pneumatic diagram (A3 PDF and SVG) | Pitot, static and TE lines are on it |

The project has no errors:
- **Warnings (21):** placeholder parts not yet verified, and the template still a draft.
- **Notes (4):** the free depth is unknown, tubes are unrouted estimates, off-panel runs are
  not set, and the endurance estimate.

**Tests:**

## The PIK-20D example project

`File › Open PIK-20D example project` opens it. `scripts/make-pik20d.ts` builds it through
the editor's own ops (`npm run gen:pik20d`), and a unit test fails if the committed file is stale.

### Front

| Part | Placement | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASI, XCVario (80 mm), XCTouchNav 5.7" (portrait), FLARM display, compass, radio, phone mount | Where the mock-up laid them out | |
| Altimeter | Moved down to the stem, as on the mock-up | |
| Master switch and four fuse holders | Either side of the altimeter, clear of the fold | Typed positions; F1 XCVario 2 A, F2 FLARM 2 A, F3 XCTouchNav 3 A, F4 radio 5 A |

There are no front errors: every cut fits the panel and clears the fold, the edges and its
neighbours.

### Rear

- **FLARM unit:** mounted on the XCTouchNav, 45–75 mm back.
- **Instrument depths:** 80 mm instruments reach 120 mm; 57 mm instruments 100 mm.
- **Tees:** three, for pitot and static.
- **Looms:** two, 130 mm back:
  - the stem loom, carrying the feeds between the exit, the switch and the fuses;
  - the top loom, a "Y" across the top section.
- **Exits:** off-panel parts leave down the stem; the speaker leaves on the right.

### Wiring and tubing

| System | Connections |
| --- | --- |
| Power | Battery → 5 A main fuse → master switch → fuse bus → each load. The XCVario, FLARM unit and display, XCTouchNav and radio each have a ground back to the battery. |
| Data | FLARM port 1 to the FLARM display; port 2 to the XCVario; the XCVario's second port to the XCTouchNav. TX to RX both ways, with signal grounds. |
| Radio | Speaker, stick PTT and VHF antenna |
| FLARM | FLARM antenna |
| Pitot | To the ASI and XCVario |
| Static | Through two tees to the ASI, altimeter and XCVario |
| TE | To the XCVario |

**Load:** 1.10 A in all (placeholder currents). On the placeholder 7.2 Ah battery that gives
about 6.5 h.

## Polish

### Onboarding

- **Welcome:** shown on a first visit (no saved work, no link). It offers three choices:
  - "Make your first panel";
  - "Explore the sample" (open already);
  - "Open the PIK-20D example".
- **First-panel walkthrough:** a card docked over the canvas, not a modal. Each step ticks
  itself off when done:
  1. pick a template (it opens the new New project dialog);
  2. place an instrument;
  3. check, then export.

  The last card points at `V`, `W` and `?`. Help › First panel walkthrough and Help ›
  Welcome screen bring each back.
- **New project dialog:**
  - shows every template with an outline thumbnail, status and accuracy;
  - takes name, registration and glider type;
  - starts on the current project's template;
  - your own templates are included, embedded whole.

### Shortcut map (`?`)

- **Layout:** grouped (Mouse, File, Export, Edit, Tools, Arrange, View, Template editor,
  Help) and searchable.
- **Rebinding:** every command can be rebound:
  1. click its key and press the new one;
  2. a key already used in the same view is refused, naming the command that has it;
  3. a changed key can be reset on its own, or all at once.

  Bindings are kept in this browser. The menus and palette show the key in use.

### Themes

- Light and dark. The default is **System**, which follows the operating system's setting as
  it changes.
- View › Toggle light / dark theme (`Shift+T`) sets an explicit choice, kept across reloads.
  View › Theme: follow system setting goes back.
- All five views were checked in dark (`img/m7-dark-*.png`).

### Crash recovery

- **Error boundaries:** the left panel, the canvas and the right panel each have one, so a
  crash in one leaves the rest working (`img/m7-crash.png`). The fallback offers: try again,
  undo the last change, download the project file, recover earlier work, or reload.
- **Autosave on the way out:** autosave now also runs at once when the tab is hidden or
  closed. A synchronous copy is kept as well, because an IndexedDB write started while the
  page closes may never finish. A test reloads straight after an edit, and the edit
  survives.
- **Snapshots:**
  - **When:** every three minutes while editing, at the start of a session, and before any
    project replaces the working copy (new, open, sample, link, recover).
  - **How many:** the newest 30 are kept.
  - **Where:** File › Recover earlier work lists them; open one, or download it as a file
    (`img/m7-recover.png`).
- **Unreadable autosave:** an autosave that no longer opens is kept whole, and can be
  downloaded instead of being overwritten.

Decisions are in [ADR 0013](../adr/0013-onboarding-recovery-preferences.md).

## Problems found and fixed while proving the reference panel

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Off-panel parts (battery, main fuse, speaker, antennas, PTT, pitot, static, TE probe) were missing from the parts library, so wiring a panel meant editing the file | Now in the library, marked "off panel". Placed at the loom depth where their wiring leaves the panel, and hidden on the front. |
| No rear layout export, though the acceptance criterion names one | Added: A3 PDF and SVG |
| Before any wiring, every powered instrument raised "power pin not connected" errors, which blocked the cut file for a front-only layout | Until the first wire (or tube) exists, open pins are one note: "Wiring not started: N parts". Every open pin is an error again once wiring starts. |
| A ground symbol drawn at a pin covered it, so clicking a grounded pin (the battery's −) could miss | Fixed |
| A net label could run over a neighbouring block, hiding its ports | Channels are now widened to fit labels from both sides. The readability test checks labels against blocks and each other, for the wiring sample and for the PIK-20D example. |
| Assigning many wires to a loom took shift-click after shift-click | The route inspector lists every wire with a tick box |
| New project silently replaced the work in hand | The work is now kept as a snapshot |
| Templates could not be edited once saved: the editor only opened drafts made from a photo, so the PIK-20D template's thickness, fixing holes or depth could not be changed | Every library template, and the project's own, now has Edit (also Project › Edit template, and a command). A shipped template is edited as your own copy; yours are edited in place. Provenance and accuracy carry over (ADR 0010 addendum). |
| Templates held component footprints (reserved areas) and existing cutouts, mixing parts into the panel's shape | Removed. A template holds only the panel; parts are placed in the project (ADR 0010 addendum). |

## Choices made without asking (rule 11)

All are listed in `docs/questions.md` (questions 23–29) for you to confirm:

- **Wiring before the first wire:** open pins are a single note, not errors (see above). It
  touches safety only in that the open-pin errors appear later, once wiring has begun.
- **The example's electrics:** the fuse ratings, the 5 A main fuse, the data links (who
  talks to whom), and the FLARM unit behind the XCTouchNav are my plausible layout, not the
  aircraft's.
- **Switch and fuse positions:** placed on the stem beside the altimeter. The mock-up does
  not show them.
- **Placeholder sizes:** for the XCTouchNav, FLARM display and phone mount, taken from the
  spaces drawn on the mock-up.
- **Off-panel runs:** left unset, because none were measured. Wire lengths therefore stop
  at the panel exit, and are flagged as such.
- **Snapshot policy:** every three minutes, 30 kept.
